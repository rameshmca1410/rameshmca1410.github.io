About

From Perumanallur

Ramesh G's native place is Perumanallur, near Ayyampalayam, in Tiruppur district, where he spent his childhood. In 2025, his family moved to a new home in Perumanallur.

He is a cheerful, jolly person who loves calm surroundings and nature. He currently works in Chennai and speaks Tamil and English.

He loves cooking South Indian food, from sambar and rasam to poriyal and curd rice, and enjoys cooking a good meal, eating together and sharing it with friends.

Hometown corner

Perumanallur

பெருமாநல்லூர்

Perumanallur, near Ayyampalayam in Tiruppur district, is where Ramesh grew up and went to school. In 2025 his family moved to a new home here.

Village temples, festivals like Vinayagar Chaturthi, and calm green surroundings are what he loves most about home.

Where every trip begins: highway mornings, roadside breaks and quiet night rides on his first bike.
