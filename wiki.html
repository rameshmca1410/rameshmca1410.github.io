Ramesh G (born 14 October 1992) is a Technology Engineer at Purpleslate in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. He grew up in Perumanallur, near Ayyampalayam in Tiruppur district, and holds an MCA from Kalaignar Karunanidhi Institute of Technology, Coimbatore.

Before joining Purpleslate in 2021, he worked for four years at Sthira IT Solutions in Bangalore. Outside work, he enjoys cricket, table tennis, music, movies and cooking South Indian food.

Early life

Ramesh spent his childhood in Perumanallur, a pleasant village near Ayyampalayam. He grew up there playing with his friends and enjoying village life.

Education

Schooling

He began his schooling at the Government School in Perumanallur. When he was in 4th standard, he watched his first movie in a theatre, The Mummy Returns. From 6th to 10th standard (2003–2007), he studied at Sakthi Vigneswara Kalvi Nilayam, which was then located at Kanakkampalayam Pirivu; the school has since moved to a new campus.

In 6th and 7th standard, he travelled to school by school van and bus. From 8th standard onwards, his family allowed him to cycle to school. At school he took part in running and volleyball, but cricket was the game he played the most.

Higher secondary

After completing 10th standard, he joined Shree Gurukulam Higher Secondary School in Gobichettipalayam for 11th and 12th standard (2007–2009). He stayed in the school hostel, which was his first time living away from his family.

Apart from his studies, he played throwball in open state-level and divisional matches, which he remembers as a very special moment. He also played cricket once a week and enjoyed the hostel food.

College

From 2009 to 2012, he studied B.Sc. Mathematics with Computer Applications at Nandha Arts and Science College, Erode. He travelled about 40 minutes by bus from his hometown each day, and those bus journeys, full of fun and chats with friends, are among his best memories.

From 2012 to 2015, he completed his MCA at Kalaignar Karunanidhi Institute of Technology, Coimbatore. Living away from his family once again, he shared a rented room with his friends, and he remembers those three years as the most enjoyable of his student life.

Career

First job in Chennai

In 2015, he moved to Chennai to look for a job. His first job was a temporary role at Tech Mahindra in Ambattur, Chennai, in a BPO voice process for Vodafone. He left the job after the Chennai floods of 2015 and attended many interviews while searching for a new role, but was not selected.

Course in Hyderabad

He then decided to take a course in Hyderabad, where courses cost much less than in Chennai, and joined one in Secunderabad in April 2017. Before the course was over, he travelled to Bangalore to attend a job interview through a friend’s reference.

Sthira IT Solutions, Bangalore

He was selected and joined Sthira IT Solutions in Bangalore in 2017, where he worked for four years. For nine months of his first year, he worked at a client site in Puducherry. He then worked from the office, and during the COVID-19 pandemic he worked from home for about a year.

Purpleslate, Chennai

In 2021, he returned to Chennai and joined Purpleslate as a Technology Engineer. He works at its office in Thiruvanmiyur.

Apart from work, he has recently taken up table tennis and enjoys playing it. He sometimes plays badminton with his friends at weekends, and plays turf cricket on weekends or weekdays whenever he can.

Personal life

Ramesh married Selvanayagam on 18 May 2026. She is from Thiruvadanai, near Sivaganga, and works as a college professor.
